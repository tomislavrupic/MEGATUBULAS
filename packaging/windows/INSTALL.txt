MEGATUBULAS 0.3.0 — Pixel Records
Windows x64. VST3 + standalone.

Close your DAW. Copy the ENTIRE MEGATUBULAS.vst3 bundle/folder to:
  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\Pixel Records
Create Pixel Records if necessary. Windows may ask for administrator approval.
Keep a backup before replacing an earlier version. Do not copy only the inner
binary; its bundle structure is required. Rescan your DAW and look for
Pixel Records / MEGATUBULAS. Confirm v0.3.0 in the editor footer.

MEGATUBULAS.exe is the optional standalone application; it does not replace
the VST3 in your DAW. AU plugins are macOS-only.

This is an unsigned development build. Windows may ask you to confirm that
you trust it. Numerical/processor checks are not a guarantee for every DAW.
No hidden limiter. Watch your levels; use Match for comparisons.
